# Clinical questions for Story 1

## Cytarabine exactly 200 mg/m²

The supplied UZA v2 page 1 says Low at **<200 mg/m²**, and Moderate at **>200**
and below 3 g/m². It does not explicitly assign exactly 200 mg/m². Which local
classification should be used at that exact boundary?

Until the clinical leads confirm a rule, Story 1 refuses to assign a final tier
at exactly 200 mg/m² and displays the source ambiguity. No POGO threshold is
silently substituted for the hospital policy.

## Route coverage

The implementation limits explicitly IV rules to IV, and the explicit IT rule
to intrathecal administration. The team should validate additional routes and
confirm how doses are recorded (per administration versus daily total) before
extending classification beyond the supplied table.
