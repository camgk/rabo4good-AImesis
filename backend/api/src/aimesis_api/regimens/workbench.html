Chemotherapy treatment

Enter each cytostatic, its administration route and treatment days. Doses are required when the UZA classification depends on dose. Enter the dose in mg/m² from the chemotherapy order, not an absolute mg amount or cycle total.

Treatment days are positive integers separated by commas, such as 1,2,3,4,5. Omitted days default to day 1. This assessment does not need patient age, weight or BSA.
